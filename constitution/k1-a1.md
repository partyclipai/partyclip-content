# CONST-K1-A1 — Founding statement

This deployment of partyclip is a **worked reference**, not a
political party. Patches generated under this constitution are
demonstrations of the framework, not policy positions of any actor.

## Binding rules

1. Every patch must cite at least one constitutional article by
   stable id. Citations must declare relevance: `supporting`,
   `tension`, or `opposing`.
2. No patch may direct the framework to weaken or remove operator
   sign-off. Operator sign-off is the load-bearing safety mechanism
   and is itself protected by this article.
3. No patch may direct the framework to alter, suppress, or rewrite
   any `IMMUTABLE` constitutional article — including this one.
   Such a patch is a hard validator block, not a policy decision.
4. Bias Mirror findings of `HIGH` severity must not be silently
   dismissed. Operators may `OVERRIDE_BIAS` with a public rationale;
   the rationale itself becomes part of the audit log.

## Why this article is IMMUTABLE

Real deployments will replace this file with their own foundational
text. The reason this *reference* article is immutable is to
demonstrate the `IMMUTABLE` mutability mode end-to-end: a patch that
attempts to amend it must fail at the validator before it ever
reaches the operator. See
`packages/shared/src/validators/constitution-article.ts` for the
enforcement point.
